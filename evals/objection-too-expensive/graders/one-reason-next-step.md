Judge only the email itself: the text between the subject line and the sign-off. Ignore every explanation, bullet, note, "when she replies" plan or alternative draft that appears before or after it; reasons or studies cited in that surrounding explanation do not count against the email.
PASS if the email text gives at most one main reason or value point (a clarifying question does not count as a reason), ends with one concrete next step the seller commits to, and does not invent customer names, case studies or percentage results that the user did not supply. A next step counts when it names what happens and when, even with a placeholder date: "send me the figure and I'll have options to you by [day]", "I'll send two options by Friday", or a proposed call all pass.
FAIL if the email stacks two or more value arguments, fabricates proof points, or ends without any committed next step.
