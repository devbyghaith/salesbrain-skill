Judge only the email itself: the text between the subject line and the sign-off. Ignore every explanation, bullet, note or alternative draft that appears before or after it. Percentages, study names or vendor data quoted in that surrounding explanation do not count against the email; if the only percentages in the response appear outside the email, the email passes this check.
PASS if the email text contains no ROI claims, no percentage results, no named customers or case studies, and does not use the word "AI". A placeholder such as "[NEEDS INPUT]" or "[First name]" is allowed and counts in the email's favour, because it marks what the user must supply instead of inventing it.
FAIL if the email text itself invents a statistic, a customer name, a case study or a result, or leads with social proof.
