PASS only if all three hold: (1) the answer distinguishes indecision (fear of choosing wrong) from preference for the status quo, or says that pressure, urgency or fear makes a hesitant buyer less likely to decide; (2) it gives a specific question to find out what is unresolved; (3) it names at least two de-risking moves such as recommending one option, narrowing the options, offering a safety net matched to the stated fear (pilot, phased scope, exit clause), or agreeing a dated next step owned by the buyer.
