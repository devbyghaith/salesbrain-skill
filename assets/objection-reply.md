# Objection reply template

Constraints baked in: diagnose before answering, one strong reason, at most 40 spoken words or 75 written
words per turn, no stacking of angles. See `references/objections.md`.

```
1. Diagnose (one question, at most 25 words)
   Classify first: indecision vs status-quo preference; freedom / change-cost / distrust motive.
   "<Clarifying question that makes them say more about the objection, not about you.>"

2. Acknowledge (one sentence)
   Reflect their words back; no "I understand, but".

3. One reason (at most two sentences)
   The single strongest, specific point for THIS motive. No second point.

4. Advance (one sentence)
   A buyer-owned next step with a date, or a question that hands them the decision.
```

Written reply skeleton:

```
<Their objection, echoed in their words.>

<One clarifying question.>

<If you must answer now: one reason, two sentences, with a number only if it is sourced.>

<Next step with a date, owned by them.>
```

Self-check:

- Did you answer before diagnosing? Move the question first.
- Count the reasons. More than one? Cut to the strongest.
- Any urgency or fear language on a buyer who said "let me think"? Remove; de-risk instead.
- Append the self-report: which motive you assumed, evidence level of any number used.
