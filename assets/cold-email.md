# Cold email template

Constraints baked in: 50–75 words, reading grade 3–7, one interest-based ask, no links or attachments on
first touch, subject 2–4 words, no "AI", no ROI claims, no social proof. See `references/outreach.md`.

```
Subject: <2–4 words, lowercase, about their situation>

<Line 1: one observation about THEM that fails the removal test if deleted (a trigger, a change, a
public statement, a specific problem their role carries).>

<Line 2: the problem you suspect, stated as a question or a hunch, in their words. No product.>

<Line 3: one interest-based ask. "Worth a look?" / "Open to hearing how <peer role> handles this?" Never a
calendar slot in the first touch.>

<Sign-off: first name only. No title block, no banner, no "P.S." pitch.>
```

Self-check before sending:

- Delete line 1. Does the email still make sense? Then it was not personalized. Rewrite.
- Word count under 75. One question mark total.
- Would the recipient know what you sell? They should not need to yet.
- Append the self-report: assumptions made, what you could not verify about the prospect.
